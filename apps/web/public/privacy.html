1. Local-First Architecture Guarantee

Welcome to MyFinanceOS. We believe your personal financial records belong solely to you. Unlike conventional cloud fintech platforms that harvest and profile bank accounts, MyFinanceOS is built entirely on a Local-First Architecture.

The Zero Server Storage Guarantee: We do not operate centralized backend databases that store, inspect, process, or sell your account balances, salary slips, investment portfolios, tax records, or transaction logs.
  • On-Device Computing: All calculations (Net Worth rollups, XIRR returns, Old vs New tax slabs, Sankey money flow graphs, EMI schedules) execute locally inside your client runtime.
  • No Central Data Store: There is no remote central database containing your unencrypted personal identity or financial records.
💾

2. Client-Side Encryption & Zero-Knowledge Storage

To enable multi-device synchronisation without exposing you to centralized cloud data breach vectors, MyFinanceOS is engineered with client-side encryption at rest and in transit.

  • Local Device Sandbox: The database is serialized and encrypted on your hardware into salt:iv:ciphertext payloads before being saved to browser localStorage.
  • Ciphertext-Only Cloud Replica: When opt-in cloud synchronization is enabled, only encrypted ciphertext blobs are uploaded. The decryption key is derived from your PIN and never leaves your device.
  • Zero Vendor Access: We physically cannot view, index, read, or sell your financial data, account balances, or tax records.
🔐

3. Authentication & Minimal Identity Scopes

When authenticating via Google OAuth for cloud sync, MyFinanceOS requests only standard identity scopes:

openid email profile — Allows basic session verification to attach your encrypted vault replica.
  • We do not request access to your Gmail, Contacts, Location, or personal files.
  • Your OAuth access tokens are held exclusively in volatile memory / encrypted local storage on your client machine.
🔑

4. AES-256 Key Encryption & Local Passcode Vault

MyFinanceOS supports custom vault passcodes to safeguard your database payload against unauthorized physical access on your device.

  • Military-Grade Cipher: Vault payloads are encrypted using AES-256 GCM authenticated encryption keys generated on-device via PBKDF2.
  • Zero-Knowledge Key Management: Your secret master passcode is never sent over any network. If you lock your workspace session, the database cannot be unlocked without entering your secret key.
🌐

5. Third-Party Services & AI Intelligence Models

We maintain strict boundaries regarding third-party service providers:

  • Static App Hosting: Web assets are served statically. Hosting providers log standard HTTPS metadata (IP address, user agent) for DDoS prevention and static asset delivery.
  • AI Assistant Privacy Options: In the AI Financial Assistant, prompts are processed locally or dispatched using your private Google Gemini API key directly to Google's API without intermediary proxy storage.
  • No Advertising or Tracking Pixels: We do not include third-party ad networks, Google Analytics, Meta Pixels, or data broker beacons inside MyFinanceOS.
⚖️

6. India Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023

MyFinanceOS complies with the statutory provisions of India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP):

  • Data Principal Sovereignty: You retain complete control over your personal data. Because unencrypted data never leaves your hardware, third-party data fiduciary risks are engineered down to zero.
  • Notice & Processing Boundaries: We only process data in your local browser for providing the software functionality you explicitly invoke.
🗑️

7. Your Rights & Total Data Export / Destruction

Because you retain 100% ownership of your data, you hold complete control:

  • 1-Click Data Export: Export your complete financial ledger and account profiles into standard JSON or CSV backups at any time.
  • Instant Data Destruction: Reset your workspace database or clear your browser cache to permanently remove all stored application state from your device.
