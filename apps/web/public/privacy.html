1. Local-First Architecture Guarantee

Welcome to MyFinanceOS. We believe your personal financial data belongs to you—and only you. Unlike traditional cloud fintech services, MyFinanceOS is built on a Local-First Privacy Architecture.

Core Promise: We do not operate central application servers that store, inspect, process, or sell your account balances, salary details, investment portfolios, tax records, or transaction logs.
  • On-Device Computing: All calculations (Net Worth, XIRR returns, tax slabs, cash flow Sankey graphs) are executed locally inside your browser or desktop sandbox.
  • No Central Database: There is no remote central database containing your personal identity or financial records.
💾

2. Data Storage & Personal Google Drive Sync

To enable seamless cross-device synchronization without centralized server risks, MyFinanceOS offers optional sync directly to your personal Google Drive account.

  • Isolated AppData Folder: The application creates a single encrypted file (financeos_db.json) inside your personal Google Drive's hidden Application Data folder (appDataFolder).
  • Complete Isolation: Our application physically cannot see, access, read, or modify any of your other Google Drive files, Google Docs, photos, or personal folders.
  • Local-First Fallback: If Google Drive sync is not configured or disabled, all database records remain saved exclusively inside your local browser's encrypted localStorage / IndexedDB storage.
🔐

3. Google OAuth Restricted Permissions Scope

When authenticating via Google Sign-In, MyFinanceOS requests only the minimum necessary restricted OAuth scope:

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata — Allows the app to create and manage its own configuration data file inside your personal Google Drive application storage space.
  • We do not request access to your Gmail, Contacts, Location, or General Google Drive storage.
  • Your OAuth access tokens are held exclusively in volatile memory / encrypted local state on your machine.
🔑

4. AES-256 Key Encryption & Local Passcode Vault

MyFinanceOS supports local vault passcodes to safeguard your database payload against unauthorized physical access on your device.

  • Military-Grade Cipher: Vault payloads are encrypted using AES-256 GCM encryption keys generated on-device.
  • Zero-Knowledge Key Management: Your secret master passcode is never sent over any network. If you lock your workspace session, the database cannot be unlocked without entering your secret key.
🌐

5. Third-Party Services & AI Intelligence Models

We maintain strict boundaries regarding third-party service providers:

  • Static App Hosting: Web assets are served statically (e.g., via Vercel / GitHub Pages). Hosting providers log standard HTTPS metadata (IP address, user agent) for DDoS prevention and static asset delivery.
  • AI Assistant Privacy Options: In the AI Financial Assistant, you can run questions locally or supply your own private Google Gemini API key. Raw database records are never transmitted to third parties without your explicit user request.
  • No Advertising or Tracking Pixels: We do not include third-party ad networks, tracking scripts, or data broker analytics inside MyFinanceOS.
⚖️

6. Your Rights & Total Data Export / Destruction

Because you retain 100% ownership of your data, you hold complete control:

  • 1-Click Data Export: Export your complete financial ledger and account profiles into standard JSON or CSV backups at any time.
  • Instant Data Destruction: Lock or reset your workspace database, or clear your browser cache, to permanently remove all stored application state from your device.
